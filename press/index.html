Describing Capsla

In one sentence

Capsla is a Pomodoro timer for iPhone and Apple Watch that runs whole focus sessions on its own, shows the countdown wherever you look and keeps distracting apps closed until focus ends.

In a paragraph

Capsla turns the Pomodoro technique into a session that runs itself: focus, a short break and the next focus follow one another without a tap until the long break, and every transition is scheduled ahead, so it keeps going with the app closed. The countdown lives in a Live Activity and the Dynamic Island, on the Lock Screen, in widgets and on Apple Watch, where its own app starts and pauses focus even without the iPhone nearby. On iPhone Duo it fits every position: the outer and the inner display, half-folded with the timer away from the fold, and tabletop, like a laptop, with the time on the top half and the buttons on the bottom. During focus, Screen Time keeps the apps you chose closed. Statistics count net focus time without pauses, and history starts the day you install the app, with nothing filled in after the fact. The core is free with no ads; Capsla+ is a one-time purchase that adds thirteen backgrounds, including Snowfall with Frost, typefaces for the timer and focus sounds.

What makes it different

  • A session that runs itselfSeparate switches start breaks and the next focus automatically. Transitions are scheduled ahead, so signals arrive on time with the app closed.
  • On the wrist, without the phoneThe Apple Watch app starts and pauses focus even when the iPhone isn’t nearby, taps the wrist at the end of a phase and counts in the iPhone’s statistics.
  • Statistics that don’t flatterNet minutes without pauses, the hours you focus best, days in a row. History starts at install and the app never fills in numbers it did not record.
  • Privacy you can checkThere is no account. The Privacy screen shows the actual events Capsla has sent from the device, and two switches turn collection off.
  • Free core, one-time extrasEverything released before Capsla+ stays free. Capsla+ adds looks and sound only, with no subscription and no pop-ups asking to buy.

What’s new in 1.7.0

  • Ten new backgrounds. Free: Night, and Sky, which follows the time of day. In Capsla+: Candle, Northern Lights, Leaf Shadows, Starlight, Snowfall, Lava Lamp, Nebula and “Life”, where a tap seeds new cells. Snowfall comes with Frost: snow falls behind the glass while ice creeps over the timer capsule as you focus; wipe it clear with a finger, and it thaws on a break
  • “Working on” (free): add a short note to your focus, like “chapter 3”. It shows on the timer, on the Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island, on Apple Watch, in Statistics, in Calendar and in the CSV export, and you can set it from Shortcuts too
  • A new way to choose your look: background tiles and a full-screen editor on the real timer. Swipe through backgrounds and change the digits, accent color and celebration in one place; Capsla+ items can be tried before you buy
  • iPhone Duo: the timer and the editor fit every position, including tabletop, like a laptop: the time on the top half, the buttons on the bottom
  • Easier on the battery: Orbits, the default background, and Frost now ask far less of the graphics
