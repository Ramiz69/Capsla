Describing Capsla

In one sentence

Capsla is a Pomodoro timer for iPhone and Apple Watch that runs whole focus sessions on its own, shows the countdown wherever you look and keeps distracting apps closed during focus.

In a paragraph

Capsla turns the Pomodoro technique into a session that runs itself: focus, a short break and the next focus follow one another without a tap until the long break, and every transition is scheduled ahead, so it keeps going with the app closed. The countdown lives in a Live Activity and the Dynamic Island, on the Lock Screen, in widgets and on Apple Watch, where its own app starts and pauses focus even without the iPhone nearby. On iPhone Duo it fits every position: the outer and the inner display, half-folded with the timer away from the fold, and tabletop, like a laptop, with the time on the top half and the buttons on the bottom. During focus, Screen Time keeps the apps you chose closed. Statistics count net focus time without pauses, and history starts the day you install the app, with nothing filled in after the fact. The core is free with no ads; Capsla+ is a one-time purchase that adds fourteen backgrounds, including Solar System with the real Earth and Snowfall with Frost, typefaces for the timer and focus sounds.

What makes it different

  • A session that runs itselfSeparate switches start breaks and the next focus automatically. Transitions are scheduled ahead, so signals arrive on time with the app closed.
  • On the wrist, without the phoneThe Apple Watch app starts and pauses focus even when the iPhone isn’t nearby, taps the wrist at the end of a phase and counts in the iPhone’s statistics.
  • Statistics that don’t flatterNet minutes without pauses, the hours you focus best, days in a row. History starts at install and the app never fills in numbers it did not record.
  • Collects nothingThere is no account, no analytics and no crash reporting of its own. Focus history stays in the user’s own iCloud.
  • Free core, one-time extrasEverything released before Capsla+ stays free. Capsla+ adds looks and sound only, with no subscription and no pop-ups asking to buy.

What’s new in 1.8.1

  • If you allow notifications on the screen that appears when focus starts, the end-of-phase notification now arrives for that same focus
  • A reminder to come back: it arrives once, five minutes after a short break ends, if the next focus hasn’t started. The permission screen offers it when you first allow notifications; if they’re already allowed, turn it on in notification settings

Also new in 1.8.0

  • Solar System in Capsla+: the view from a porthole. Under the capsule is the real Earth from NASA maps, with city lights on the night side. As you focus, the planet slowly drifts away; on a break the Sun rises over its edge. Choose from ten bodies, from Mercury to Neptune, the Moon and the Sun itself, each with its own Sky Event at the start of a long break. With Device time, light and shadow match the real sky right now
  • Two new celebrations in Capsla+: Fireflies and Ripples
  • A new line under the timer every launch, without repeating recent ones. The Phrase of the Day widget (free) brings a new line every day to the Home Screen and the Lock Screen
  • Apple Intelligence, on device, where it’s available: Capsla suggests a tag from what you’re working on, groups similar notes in Statistics, and writes a short review of your week
  • Capsla no longer collects anything: analytics and crash reporting are gone. If something breaks, you can attach a crash report to an email yourself
  • iPhone Duo: Settings and Statistics in two columns, reset and skip in the side bar, and Solar System adapts to the screen and the fold
  • Starlight and Northern Lights are now much easier on the graphics
