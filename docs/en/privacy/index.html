Introduction

Capsla is a Pomodoro timer app designed to help users stay focused and manage time effectively.

We respect your privacy. Capsla is designed to collect as little information as possible.

Data We Collect

Capsla does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

The app may collect anonymous usage data and crash reports through third-party services to improve performance and reliability.

This data may include:

  • app interactions (e.g. session duration, feature usage)
  • device type, OS version, and general region
  • crash logs and diagnostics

This information does not identify you personally.

How We Use Data

Any locally stored data is used only to provide app functionality, such as:

  • saving timer duration
  • remembering your preferences
  • improving your user experience inside the app

iCloud Sync

If you are signed in to your Apple Account and iCloud is enabled, your phase history and focus tags sync across your devices.

What syncs:

  • records of completed phases: phase type, planned and actual duration, start and end time, cycle number, outcome and time zone
  • focus tags: title, color, icon and order

This data goes into the private database of your own iCloud account. The developer of Capsla has no access to it and cannot read it: syncing is handled by Apple under its own terms — Apple Privacy Policy.

Your selection of apps to block does not sync — it stays on the device.

You can turn syncing off in the system iCloud settings by disabling Capsla there. Your data stays on the device.

Screen Time

Capsla can block selected apps while you focus. This requires Screen Time access, and it is requested only when you turn blocking on.

Apps are chosen in Apple’s own system dialog. Capsla receives opaque tokens from the system and does not see the names of the apps you selected — it has no way of learning what you picked.

The tokens are stored on your device only. They are not sent to the developer, not shared with third parties, and not included in iCloud sync.

Capsla does not request or read Screen Time statistics — how much time you spend in apps is unknown to it.

You can revoke access in the system settings, under Screen Time. Deleting the app deletes your app selection along with it.

Third-Party Services

Capsla uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Analytics — for anonymous usage analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics — for crash reporting and diagnostics

These services are provided by Google LLC and may collect anonymous information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

You can learn more about how Google handles data here:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Capsla does not use this data to personally identify users.

Data Retention

Data stored locally on your device remains there until you delete the app or clear the app’s data, if such an option is available.

If iCloud sync is on, a copy of your data remains in your iCloud account even after you delete the app. You can remove it in the system iCloud settings, under account storage management.

Children’s Privacy

Capsla does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We aim to keep the app simple and privacy-friendly. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
