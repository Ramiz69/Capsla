A quiet timer for focus and breaks

A focus phase, a short break, and after a few cycles a long one. You set the phase lengths and the number of cycles yourself.

Download on the App Store

Free, no ads. iPhone, iOS 26 or later.

What Capsla does

Your own rhythm, not 25/5

Focus, short break and long break can be any length, from 1 to 999 minutes. The number of cycles before a long break is chosen from 1 to 10. You can add 1 or 5 minutes to a focus already running.

See the timer without opening the app

A Live Activity keeps the phase on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island: pause, reset, and skip during a break. The same card reaches Apple Watch, where it only shows the time. Control Center has a toggle that starts and stops a focus.

  • Notifications

    The app tells you a phase has ended, warns you that a focus is about to finish, and reminds you to come back if your break ran long. You can start a break or skip it straight from the notification.

  • Widgets

    Timer and statistics widgets for the Home Screen, plus “Focus today” on the Lock Screen.

  • Siri and Shortcuts

    Ask Siri to start the timer, pause it or skip a break — and it will tell you how much is left. The answer comes with a card of buttons, so you can keep going right there.

  • Tags

    A tag marks what you are doing: work, study, sport — or your own. Tags show up in search on your phone, and when a Focus mode is on, Capsla fills in the right tag by itself.

  • Social apps stay shut during a focus

    Chosen apps can be blocked for the length of a focus — Screen Time does the blocking.

  • Statistics

    Every finished phase goes into the history: net time, tag, hour of the day. From there come days in a row, breaks skipped and the “when you focus” chart. History exports to CSV, and iCloud syncs it between your devices.

  • You can see what leaves the phone

    Two switches for data collection and a log of sent events: you can look at what exactly was sent and turn collection off.

  • Sound and haptics

    The end-of-phase sound plays even in silent mode — otherwise the switch is easy to miss. There are 16 sounds to choose from. Haptics can be adjusted or turned off inside the app: three patterns, different for the end of a focus and the end of a break.

  • Quick actions on the icon

    A long press on the app icon opens quick actions — start a focus without waiting for the screen to load.

  • Accessibility and languages

    Dark mode, increased contrast and larger text are supported. The interface is in Russian, English and German.

Four animated backgrounds

Gradient, Orbits, Waves and Breathing. Gradient has six styles, Waves has a colour of its own, Orbits has ring density and five palettes: monochrome and four in colour.

Not only for desk work

The timer helps wherever a rhythm has to come from outside: focusing with ADHD, counting sets at the gym, timing turns in party games.

No ads, no paywalled buttons

The core features are free and will stay free. There are no screens offering you something to buy, and no buttons that stop working without payment.

An iPhone is all you need

Any iPhone with iOS 26 or later. The app is free, and there is no sign-up.
